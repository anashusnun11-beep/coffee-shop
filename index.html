Our Story

From a garage roaster to your corner café.

Ember & Bean started in 2014 with a secondhand roaster in Maya and Leo's garage and a farmers' market stall that sold out by 10am. A decade later we're still roasting a few kilos at a time, still learning, and still obsessed with getting each cup just right.

Our café is a place for laptops and long chats, first dates and neighbors catching up. The wifi is fast, the playlist is good, and there's always a seat by the window if you wait a minute.
